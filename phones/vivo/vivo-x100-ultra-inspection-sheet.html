Read this first: how this phone differs

  • It is China software. Every X100 Ultra is a China unit running China OriginOS. Google apps are added afterwards, and some apps may treat the phone as uncertified.
  • No official bootloader unlock. Vivo offers none, so unlike a Xiaomi, a modified phone came from an unofficial method. The sheet asks the seller directly.
  • Notifications are the main risk. China power management can delay alerts, especially overnight. The sheet adds an idle test and a written trial agreement.
  • Dropped from the Xiaomi sheet: the MIXM region code, CE label and Mi Account checks. Replaced with vivo account and Google checks.
  • Not verified. Whether banking apps in Bangladesh accept the phone, and how VoLTE behaves on each local operator. Test both in person.
