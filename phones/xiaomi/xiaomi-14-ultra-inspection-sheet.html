What I changed from the earlier checklist

  • Fingerprint. The earlier list said the 14 Ultra has an ultrasonic scanner. Published specs list an optical under-screen scanner, so an optical scanner is normal and is not a sign of a fake screen.
  • Fastboot "LOCKED" text. I could not confirm that the Xiaomi fastboot screen prints LOCKED. Xiaomi's own FAQ says to check Developer options, where Mi Unlock status is shown, and that an unlocked phone shows an unlocked notice at startup. The sheet uses those two checks.
  • mi.com/global/verify. I could not confirm this page exists, so it is removed. Use the model number, IMEI lookup and Play Protect checks instead.
  • Call recorder toggle. Dropped. Its presence does not reliably identify a region.
  • Added. Bangladesh NEIR registration (a phone can be blocked on the network), Google account lock, financed-phone lock apps, delayed-notification test, Play Protect certification, RAM and storage check.
